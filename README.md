# ClickKit

### Everyday digital tools, without the fuss.

ClickKit is a collection of small, practical tools for the digital tasks that turn up every day: counting and reshaping words, checking a sum, converting a measurement, tidying a PDF, or getting a quick answer without signing up or working through a complicated workflow.

Every tool does one job and gets out of the way. There are no accounts, no uploads, and no waiting in a queue — the work happens in your browser, so whatever you drop in stays on your own device.

It is also a catalogue that keeps growing. New tools arrive most weeks, and the ones added recently are marked as new so you can see what changed.

## What is inside

- **Text & writing** — count, clean, compare, and convert words, run ciphers, spot invisible characters, and get a feel for how a piece of writing reads.
- **Calculators** — everyday sums and scientific ones, percentages and discounts, loans and savings, bills and time, unit conversions, dates, and health measures like BMI.
- **Developer tools** — formats, schemas, hashes, encodings, queries, subnets, permissions, colours, cron, and the small utilities that save a trip to a terminal.
- **Image & files** — resize, compress, crop, convert, watermark, and inspect pictures, build QR codes and favicons, and pack or split files.
- **PDF & documents** — merge, split, organise, rotate, compress, watermark, number, unlock, trim, and edit PDFs, and turn them into images or plain text.
- **Converters** — move writing and notes between formats: Markdown, Word, HTML, RTF, subtitles, contacts, calendars, spreadsheets, and slides.
- **Productivity** — passwords, focus timers, picking fairly from a list, balanced teams, and finding a meeting hour across time zones.

## Getting to a tool

Search the full catalogue, filter it by category, or open a tool by name. Each tool keeps its own page, so a bookmark or a shared link goes straight back to it.

ClickKit can be installed from the browser and keeps working when the connection drops, which is useful for the tools you reach for on a phone.

## Recent changes

The changelog lists what landed and when, grouped by day and newest first. Anything inside its first couple of weeks is marked **New** in the catalogue and on its own page.
